# Datasets Catalog

Public demo datasets for cuvis-ai, hosted on
[HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/cubert-gmbh). Each entry below
mirrors the dataset's HuggingFace README; the HF page is the
authoritative source.

All three are captured with a Cubert
[**Ultris XMR**](https://cubert-hyperspectral.com/de/ultris-xmr/)
hyperspectral camera (61 bands, 430–910 nm).

## Available datasets

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-water: **[XMR_Demo_Blood_Perfusion](XMR_Demo_Blood_Perfusion.md)**

    ---

    Tissue oxygenation visualised via two-band differential. Pairs with the [Blood Perfusion tutorial](../../tutorials/statistical/blood-perfusion.md).

-   :material-food: **[XMR_Demo_Industrial_Foreign_Object_Detection_Lentils](XMR_Demo_Industrial_Foreign_Object_Detection_Lentils.md)**

    ---

    Lentil-conveyor scene with stones, stems, aluminium shards, and flies. Pairs with the [AdaCLIP tutorial](../../tutorials/gradient/adaclip.md).

-   :material-account-group: **[XMR_Demo_Object_Tracking](XMR_Demo_Object_Tracking.md)**

    ---

    Crowded bus-station scene with passive and active (invisible-ink) tracking modes.
