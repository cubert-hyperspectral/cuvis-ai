# Datasets Catalog

Public demo datasets for cuvis-ai, hosted on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/cubert-gmbh). Each entry below mirrors the dataset's HuggingFace README; the HF page is the authoritative source.

All three are captured with a Cubert [**Ultris XMR**](https://cubert-hyperspectral.com/de/ultris-xmr/) hyperspectral camera (61 bands, 430–910 nm).

## Available datasets

- **[XMR_Demo_Blood_Perfusion](https://cubert-hyperspectral.github.io/cuvis-ai/0.10.2/catalogs/datasets/XMR_Demo_Blood_Perfusion/index.md)**

  ______________________________________________________________________

  Tissue oxygenation visualised via two-band differential. Pairs with the [Blood Perfusion tutorial](https://cubert-hyperspectral.github.io/cuvis-ai/0.10.2/tutorials/statistical/blood-perfusion/index.md).

- **[XMR_Demo_Industrial_FOD_Lentils](https://cubert-hyperspectral.github.io/cuvis-ai/0.10.2/catalogs/datasets/XMR_Demo_Industrial_FOD_Lentils/index.md)**

  ______________________________________________________________________

  Lentil-conveyor scene with stones, stems, aluminium shards, and flies. Pairs with the [AdaCLIP tutorial](https://cubert-hyperspectral.github.io/cuvis-ai/0.10.2/tutorials/gradient/adaclip/index.md).

- **[XMR_Demo_Object_Tracking](https://cubert-hyperspectral.github.io/cuvis-ai/0.10.2/catalogs/datasets/XMR_Demo_Object_Tracking/index.md)**

  ______________________________________________________________________

  Crowded bus-station scene with passive and active (invisible-ink) tracking modes.
